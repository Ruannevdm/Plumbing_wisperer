Signs you may have a leak

  • Your water bill jumpedwithout a change in how you use water.
  • The meter keeps turningwith every tap in the house closed.
  • Damp patches or bubbling painton walls, ceilings or floors.
  • Low water pressureor the sound of running water when nothing is on.
