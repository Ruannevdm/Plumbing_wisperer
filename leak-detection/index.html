Signs of a hidden leak

Most leaks are out of sight: underground, under paving, inside walls or above the ceiling.

  • Your water bill jumpedwithout a change in how you use water.
  • The meter keeps turningwith every tap in the house closed.
  • Damp patches or bubbling painton walls, ceilings or floors.
  • Low water pressureor the sound of running water when nothing is on.
  • Wet or sunken spotsin the garden, driveway or paving.
  • Stains on the ceilingbelow the geyser or a bathroom.

Where leaks hide

We find leaks in homes, sectional-title complexes, estates, factories and industrial buildings.

  • Underground supply pipesbetween the meter and the house.
  • Under paving and drivewayswhere water soaks away unseen.
  • Inside walls and under floorsbehind tiles, cupboards and slabs.
  • Geysers and geyser pipesin the roof, above the ceiling.
  • Toilets and cisternsthat run quietly day and night.
  • Common propertyin complexes, where one leak raises the shared bill.

Questions

Do you have to break open walls or paving?

We pinpoint the leak first so we only open up where we need to. Afterwards we repair the ceiling, wall, paint or paving so you are not left with a hole.

What should I do if a pipe bursts?

Close the main water valve, usually next to the water meter, and switch off the geyser at the distribution board if the geyser is leaking. Then call us on 068 299 0549. We come out 24/7 for emergencies.
